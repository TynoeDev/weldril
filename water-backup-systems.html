Reliable supply

Backup systems should be sized around real daily water demand.

A small home, multi-unit complex and commercial property all need different storage, refill and pressure planning. The system should solve the outage, not create new pressure and maintenance issues.

  • Municipal backup systems for load-shedding and supply interruption resilience.
  • Borehole-to-tank systems that store water before pressure boosting.
  • Booster pump installations for showers, irrigation, kitchens and commercial supply.
  • Filtration options for sediment, odour, taste and borehole water quality concerns.
